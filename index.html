A learning philosophy

Attention is a material we can care for.

Early learning begins before a formal curriculum: a leaf, a question, a sound, a pattern, a story, a place.

We make those beginnings legible without flattening them. A child can notice, name, compare, draw, move, ask, and return to the question later.

Castalia favors bounded activities, physical reference points, family control, and tools that remain understandable and repairable.
